# Geometry V3 Protocol

Protocol ID: `geometry-v3-2026-08-19`

Status: **document frozen as of this commit.** The scenario family, the
discovery/confirmation split rule, the candidate predictor menu, the selection
rule, the confirmation criterion, and the failure semantics below are closed.
They may not be altered under this protocol ID.

Freeze stages:

1. **Document frozen (this commit).** Protocol only.
2. **Structure frozen.** Generator, configs, evaluator, and tests committed.
   The discovery/confirmation split is computed and committed **before any
   scenario is executed**, so membership cannot be adjusted after seeing
   results.
3. **Predictor frozen.** The predictor selected on the discovery set is
   committed together with the complete discovery record.

Only after stage 3 may the confirmation set be executed.

## Purpose

`geometry-v2-2026-08-18` established that RWP-OMD's advantage over a
step-swept uniform frontier is real -- positive on 6 of 8 scenarios, from
+0.0036 to +0.1228 after bias correction -- and **refuted** the hypothesis that
a pure-critical tie-group is what produces it. An exactly-matched arm with
`pure_crit = 0` and homogeneous yield ratios measured +0.00494
[+0.00418, +0.00570] where both pre-registered hypotheses predicted null.

No replacement law was asserted, because the graded pattern visible in that
data is post-hoc and the eight-scenario set is spent for hypothesis testing.

This protocol asks:

> Is there a structural property of the feature map, computable a priori, that
> predicts the magnitude of RWP-OMD's frontier advantage?

The question matters beyond this repository. The deferred language-model phase
was designed around a cheap pre-flight screen on Qwen: measure a continuous
analogue of the structural property and stop before training if it is absent.
`pure_crit` was that property, and it is refuted, so the screen currently has
no basis. Either a predictor is found here or the language-model phase proceeds
without an early stop.

## Method: discovery and confirmation on disjoint scenarios

The failure this design exists to prevent is the one `geometry-v2` exposed:
a hypothesis read off a small set of scenarios, then tested on scenarios that
did not isolate it. Accordingly:

- A **scenario family** is generated randomly under fixed structural
  constraints, large enough that no single scenario drives a conclusion.
- The family is split into **discovery** and **confirmation** subsets by a
  committed RNG seed, **before any scenario is executed**.
- Predictor selection uses the discovery subset **only**.
- The selected predictor is frozen and committed.
- The confirmation subset is executed **once**, and its criterion is fixed
  here.

The confirmation subset is not inspected, plotted, or summarized before stage 3
is committed. Any result produced from it earlier voids the protocol.

## Scenario family

Generated by rejection sampling under these constraints, all fixed:

- `H` drawn uniformly from `{8, 12, 16}`; `A = 3`.
- A random partition of `H` positions into tie-groups, each group assigned
  `(c, d)` counts.
- At least 2 tie-groups; at least 2 critical and at least 2 distractor
  positions.
- **Realizable**: generated by the `geometry-v2` rule, in which critical
  positions within a tie-group always share a target action.
- **Learnable**: the uniform baseline at `eta = 0.75` must gain at least
  `+0.35` expected success probability over 400 iterations, measured on 5
  seeds disjoint from all evaluation seeds. This threshold is set below every
  value observed in `geometry-v2` (+0.5918 to +0.8890) and well above the
  `+0.10` that forced the `geom_dev_separable` redesign.
- **Novel**: no `(c, d)` profile appearing in `geometry-v1` or `geometry-v2`
  may be reused, at any horizon.
- **Distinct**: no two family members share a `(c, d)` profile.

Family size: **50 scenarios**, split **30 discovery / 20 confirmation** by
`numpy.random.default_rng(20260819)` permutation, committed at stage 2.

Rationale for the split sizes: 30 gives predictor selection a usable spread of
structures, and 20 is the smallest confirmation set for which the criterion
below has adequate power at the effect size sought.

## Metric

The `geometry-v2` primary metric, **with the leave-one-out bias correction now
pre-registered** rather than applied post hoc. This implements the forward rule
of [E10](ERRATA.md).

Per scenario:

1. Compute `Delta_frontier` exactly as in `geometry-v2`: per seed, bracket the
   candidate's distractor KL by KL rank among the uniform grid, interpolate
   uniform AUC linearly in log distractor KL, take the paired difference.
2. Compute the **leave-one-out bias**: for each uniform grid point, remove it,
   interpolate its own AUC from the remaining points at its own distractor KL,
   and average the resulting per-seed differences. The true value is exactly
   zero by construction, so the mean over grid points estimates the
   interpolation bias for that scenario.
3. The corrected metric is `Delta_corrected = Delta_frontier - bias`.

`Delta_corrected` is the response variable throughout. Confidence intervals are
paired 95% t-intervals on the per-seed `Delta_frontier`, shifted by the bias,
which treats the bias as a fixed per-scenario offset. **This understates
uncertainty**, because the bias is itself estimated; the limitation is recorded
here rather than hidden, and the confirmation criterion is a rank correlation,
which is insensitive to it.

Grid, methods, hyperparameters, and seeds are inherited unchanged from
`geometry-v2`: 18-point `eta` grid from 0.025 to 1.25, `rwp_eta125_lam3` with
`lambda = 3.0` and `mu = 0.0`, 20 seeds per cell. Evaluation seeds are
`600-619`, disjoint from every prior protocol.

**Grid coverage gate.** Any scenario with more than 2 dropped seeds is excluded
from the family and replaced during generation, before the split. No scenario
may be excluded after execution.

## Candidate predictor menu

Fixed here. Selection may only choose from this list; no predictor may be
invented after seeing results, and no combination of two or more is permitted,
because a family of 50 will not support fitting.

Let critical-bearing groups be those with `c(g) > 0`, and yield ratio be
`c(g)/d(g)`, taken as `+inf` when `d(g) = 0`.

| # | name | definition |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `pure_crit` | count of groups with `c>0, d=0` |
| 2 | `pure_crit_indicator` | `1[pure_crit >= 1]` |
| 3 | `alpha` | `1 - sum_g abs(c-d) / H` |
| 4 | `distinct_yield_ratios` | number of distinct yield-ratio values |
| 5 | `pure_distractor_mass` | fraction of distractor positions in groups with `c=0` |
| 6 | `critical_mass_in_pure_groups` | fraction of critical positions in groups with `d=0` |
| 7 | `n_groups` | tie-group count |
| 8 | `min_inverse_yield` | `min` over critical-bearing groups of `d(g)/c(g)` |
| 9 | `allocation_headroom` | `(sum_g d(g) / sum_g c(g)) - min_inverse_yield` |
| 10 | `log_allocation_headroom` | `log((sum_g d / sum_g c + 1e-6) / (min_inverse_yield + 1e-6))` |
| 11 | `cone_bound` | `1 / max_g c(g)`, the Lemma 2 slope |
| 12 | `group_size_dispersion` | standard deviation of tie-group sizes |

Entries 1 and 2 are refuted by `geometry-v2` and are retained deliberately: if
the selection procedure ranks either of them first on the discovery set, that is
evidence the procedure overfits, and it must be reported.

Entries 8-11 are the theoretically motivated candidates. Under assumptions
(M1)-(M3) of [`GEOMETRY_V2_PROTOCOL.md`](GEOMETRY_V2_PROTOCOL.md), critical and
distractor KL are non-negative linear functions of per-group movement, so the
best distractor-KL-per-critical-KL obtainable by re-weighting is
`min_inverse_yield`, while moving every group together gives the aggregate
ratio. Their difference is the headroom re-weighting has to exploit.

## Selection rule (discovery set only)

Deterministic:

1. For each candidate, compute the Spearman rank correlation `rho` between the
   predictor and `Delta_corrected` across the 30 discovery scenarios.
2. Rank candidates by `abs(rho)` descending; ties broken by menu order
   ascending.
3. The top-ranked candidate is selected **only if** `abs(rho) >= 0.70`.
   Otherwise the outcome is **NO PREDICTOR FOUND**, the confirmation set is not
   executed, and the language-model pre-flight screen is recorded as having no
   basis.

Rank correlation, not linear fit: the relationship's functional form is unknown,
magnitudes span two orders of magnitude, and only ordering is needed for a
screening gate.

## Confirmation criterion (confirmation set, executed once)

The selected predictor is applied to the 20 confirmation scenarios.

- **CONFIRMED** iff the Spearman `rho` between predictor and `Delta_corrected`
  has a 95% bootstrap confidence lower bound **strictly greater than 0.50**,
  with 10000 resamples at `numpy.random.default_rng(20260820)`, and the sign of
  `rho` matches the discovery set.
- **NOT CONFIRMED** otherwise.

A predictor that is CONFIRMED may be used as the basis of the language-model
pre-flight screen. A predictor that is NOT CONFIRMED may not, and the
language-model phase proceeds without an early stop or does not proceed.

The threshold 0.50 is chosen because the gate's purpose is screening, not
explanation: a predictor that orders scenarios correctly half the time better
than chance is useful for deciding whether to spend GPU weeks, whereas a
predictor that merely correlates weakly is not.

## Negative controls

1. **Complete aliasing.** At least one family member must have `alpha = 1`.
   Its secondary allocation ratio must equal `1.0` to within `1e-9`, as the
   parameterization requires. Violation is an implementation defect and forces
   **HALT**.
2. **Bias-correction validity.** The pooled leave-one-out bias across the family
   must be positive, consistent with [E10](ERRATA.md). If it is not, the
   concavity account is wrong and the correction is unjustified; the protocol
   reports **HALT** rather than a predictor.
3. **Overfitting check.** If a refuted predictor (`pure_crit` or
   `pure_crit_indicator`) is selected on the discovery set, this is reported
   prominently whatever the confirmation outcome.

## What this protocol cannot establish

- **Nothing causal.** A confirmed predictor is a correlate computable from the
  feature map. It is not a mechanism, and the protocol does not claim one.
- **Nothing outside softmax-linear one-hot tie-group features**, assumption
  (M1). Whether any predictor found here survives non-linear function
  approximation is the separate MLP question and is not answered here.
- **Nothing about language models.** A confirmed predictor licenses *building*
  a continuous analogue and measuring it on a transformer. It does not license
  assuming that analogue behaves the same way.
- **Nothing about `lambda` or `mu`**, which stay frozen at the `geometry-v1`
  values.
- **No guarantee a predictor exists.** NO PREDICTOR FOUND is a defined and
  informative outcome, not a failure of execution.
